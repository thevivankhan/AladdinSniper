Aladdin Genie Extension — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 13, 2026

Aladdin Genie is a browser overlay assistant designed to display local technical chart readings, stake formatting synchronization, and trading workflow utilities on supported Pocket Option trading interfaces.

1. Data Collection & Storage

  • Aladdin Genie does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personally identifiable information (PII), browsing history, passwords, or payment details.
  • All UI layout settings (window size, position, timeframe/expiry selection, and session counters) are stored strictly locally in your own browser via localStorage and chrome.storage.
  • Real-time chart readings and DOM element synchronization occur locally within your active browser tab.

2. Permissions Usage

  • storage: Used exclusively to save user overlay preferences and session counters locally on your device.
  • Host Permissions (Pocket Option domains): Used solely to render the floating Aladdin Genie overlay and synchronize visible trading controls on the active Pocket Option tab.

3. Third-Party Sharing

We do not transfer or sell any user data to third parties, advertisers, or analytics networks.

4. Contact

For questions regarding this privacy policy or support, please contact the extension publisher through the official store listing page.
